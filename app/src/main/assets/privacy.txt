CCompiler Pro - privacy statement

What the app does NOT do
  - It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network.
  - It asks for no permissions at all.
  - It has no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Where your data lives
  - Your code, settings and the learned order of the symbol buttons stay on this phone, in the app's private storage.
  - Backups are only created when you choose "Back up all projects", and you choose where the file is saved.
  - If the app crashes, a short technical log (error text, no code and no files) is saved on this phone. It is attached to a bug-report email only if you decide to send one.

Things that happen only when you tap them
  - Links (website, donate, community, review, releases, APK download) open in your browser or WhatsApp.
  - "Report a Bug" opens your email app with a draft you can edit or cancel.
  - "Share Code" opens Android's share sheet.

Deleting your data
  - "Clear temporary files" removes only temporary files.
  - "Delete all app data" erases everything, after a 2-minute countdown that you can cancel.
